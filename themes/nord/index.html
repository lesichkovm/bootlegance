Nord

An Arctic-based palette of cool blues and slate grays, built for focus and reduced eye strain. Four official styles from one 16-colour system.

Styles

Nord ships four official styles. Polar Night and Frost are dark, Snow Storm is light, Aurora uses Nord's alternative scheme.
